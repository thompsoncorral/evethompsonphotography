Frequently Asked Questions

How long will my order take to arrive?

Every item is made to order, so please allow a little extra time compared to a store shelf. Most products are printed within 2–7 business days (mugs, posters, and accessories are usually quickest; apparel and larger wall art can take closer to the higher end), then ship via USPS, UPS, or FedEx, adding another 3–8 business days within the US. You'll get an email with tracking as soon as it's on its way.

What if my item arrives damaged, misprinted, or wrong?

That's on me to make right — just email me a photo of the issue within 30 days of delivery and I'll get you a free replacement or a full refund, no need to send anything back.

Can I return or exchange an item if I change my mind or ordered the wrong size?

Because each piece is printed specifically for your order, I'm not able to offer returns or exchanges for a change of mind or an incorrect size/color selection. Please double-check sizing before ordering — I'm always happy to help you choose if you're unsure, just reach out first.

Do you have an address I can ship a return to?

Please contact me before sending anything back. Orders are printed and shipped by my fulfillment partner, so I'm not able to accept unannounced returns at their warehouse — if a return or replacement is ever needed, reach out first and I'll give you the right instructions.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — most items can ship both within the US and to many countries abroad. Exact shipping cost and delivery estimate are calculated at checkout once you enter your address.

How do I track my order?

As soon as your order ships you'll receive an email with a tracking link, so you can follow it the whole way to your door.

How do I see sizes and color options for a product?

Tap "Choose options" on any product to see every available size, color, and variant along with its price.
